INSTITUTION: Bharat College of Engineering
DOCUMENT: Academic Regulations, Attendance, and Lab Safety Guidelines (Academic Year 2026-2027)

SECTION 1: ATTENDANCE NORMS
1.1 Minimum Attendance Requirement:
Every undergraduate student registered for Bachelor of Engineering programs must maintain a minimum overall attendance of 75% across all scheduled theory lectures and practical laboratory sessions.

1.2 Condonation Policy:
If a student's attendance falls between 60% and 74.9%, condonation may be considered by the Academic Dean only on genuine medical grounds or sanctioned institutional representation (sports, technical symposiums, university competitions). A certified medical certificate or official permission slip must be submitted to the Department Academic Coordinator within 7 working days of resuming classes. Attendance below 60% cannot be condoned under any circumstances, and the student will be detained from the end-semester examinations for that term.

SECTION 2: LABORATORY CONDUCT & SAFETY
2.1 AI and High-Performance Computing (HPC) Lab Rules:
- The HPC lab contains dedicated GPU clusters for artificial intelligence, computer vision, and NLP research.
- Lab working hours are strictly from 8:30 AM to 5:30 PM, Monday through Friday.
- Personal USB flash drives or external bootable media are strictly prohibited on the GPU server nodes to avoid malware injection.
- Unattended batch training jobs exceeding 4 continuous hours require prior written authorization from the Lab In-Charge (Prof. K. Sharma, Room 402).
- Food, beverages, and open water bottles are strictly prohibited inside the lab enclosure.
